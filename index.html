01 / About

I trained in pharmacy first. Six years of B.Pharm and M.Pharm taught me how a drug is built into a tablet or a microsphere, and how that choice changes what happens inside the body. In 2024 I moved to IIT Guwahati for an M.Tech in biomedical engineering and started working on the material itself. I made biodegradable films from banana pseudostem nanocellulose and carbon quantum dots, and that work became my first paper.

03 / Now

Polymers change under force. Chains stretch, break and sometimes react. My PhD, under Dr. Aritra Chatterjee, looks at this chemistry of force in polymers and asks how it can be used in drug delivery and 3D Printing.

I have pulled for my team in tug of war twice and won gold once. I took drawing courses and finished several certificate programmes with distinction. I read story books, and I travel when I can.
