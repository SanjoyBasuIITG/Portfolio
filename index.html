About Me

I am passionate about the intersection of engineering and biology. My work focuses on creating biodegradable materials and leveraging nanotechnology to improve targeted drug delivery systems.

With a strong background in materials characterization and pharmaceutics, I aim to bridge the gap between benchside research and clinical applications.

Research Focus

Targeted Drug Delivery

Designing smart, biodegradable nanoparticle systems that can deliver therapeutics directly to diseased cells while minimizing off-target effects. Utilizing advanced characterization to ensure efficacy.

3D Bioprinting

Engineering complex tissue constructs using advanced bioinks. This work aims to create viable tissue models for drug testing and ultimately, regenerative medicine applications.

Cell Mechanobiology

Investigating how physical forces and changes in the mechanical properties of cells and tissues contribute to development, physiology, and disease progression.
